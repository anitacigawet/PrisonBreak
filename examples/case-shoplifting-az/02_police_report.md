### NARRATIVE

On 03/15/2026 at approximately 20:35 hrs, I was dispatched to Mohave Market at 1234 AZ-95 in reference to a shoplifting in progress. The reporting party was identified as **Marcus Park**, employed as the on-duty Loss Prevention Officer ("LP") for Mohave Market.

Upon arrival at 20:42 hrs, I made contact with LP Park in the front-of-store office. LP Park advised that he had been monitoring the store's CCTV system from the office and had observed an individual, later identified as Jamie M. Reyes (DOB redacted, address per citation), in the electronics aisle. LP Park stated that on the monitor he observed Reyes "place items into a backpack" before walking past the registers and exiting the store without paying. LP Park stated he then exited the office, made physical contact with Reyes in the parking lot, and detained Reyes pending police arrival.

I made contact with Reyes, who was seated in the front-of-store office under LP Park's supervision. Reyes was cooperative. I observed a black backpack on the floor next to Reyes containing: one (1) sealed package of wireless earbuds (Brand: Hexcomm) and one (1) USB-C charging cable. Both items bore Mohave Market price tags ($89.99 and $24.99 respectively).

I read Reyes their Miranda rights from my issued card at 20:51 hrs. Reyes acknowledged understanding and agreed to speak with me without an attorney present.

In the office, I conducted a brief interview with Reyes. (See attached interview notes — Document 05.)

After interview, I cited Reyes for shoplifting under A.R.S. § 13-1805(A)(1), Class 1 Misdemeanor. Reyes was transported to BCPD for booking and released on their own recognizance pending Initial Appearance.

The recovered merchandise was photographed in the office and returned to Mohave Market. LP Park provided a written statement (Document 04).

The store's full surveillance footage was not retrieved by me at the scene. LP Park advised that the system retains footage for thirty (30) days; I noted this in my report.

End of narrative.

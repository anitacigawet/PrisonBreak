Counselor:

Pursuant to my discussion with you at the pretrial conference of April 29, 2026, the State extends the following plea offer in the above-captioned matter. This offer is conditional upon the Defendant's acceptance in open court no later than the offer expiration date.

### TERMS

1. **Plea:** The Defendant shall plead guilty (or *no contest*, at the State's option) to **Count 1: Shoplifting, A.R.S. § 13-1805(A)(1), a Class 1 Misdemeanor**, as charged.

2. **Sentencing recommendation (joint):**
    - Twelve (12) months of unsupervised probation;
    - Restitution to Mohave Market in the amount of $114.98 (acknowledged paid at point of incident; restitution stipulation pro forma);
    - Court costs and standard misdemeanor fees;
    - Eight (8) hours of community service to be completed within ninety (90) days;
    - No incarceration recommended.

3. **Dismissal:** No counts to dismiss (single-count complaint).

4. **Stipulations:**
    - Defendant agrees not to enter Mohave Market premises during the probationary term except with prior written consent of the store.
    - Defendant agrees to waive any motion to suppress and any pretrial motion practice.
    - Defendant retains the right to set aside the conviction under A.R.S. § 13-905 upon successful completion of probation.

### DEADLINE AND ACCEPTANCE

If the Defendant wishes to accept this offer, please notify our office in writing or via the discovery portal not later than the expiration date above. Acceptance of the plea will be tendered in open court before Judge Albright at the next available change-of-plea calendar.

If the offer is rejected or expires unaccepted, the State reserves the right to proceed to trial, to seek any lawful sentence upon conviction at trial, and to amend or withdraw this offer at any time.

This offer is made in good faith and reflects the State's assessment of the case at this time.

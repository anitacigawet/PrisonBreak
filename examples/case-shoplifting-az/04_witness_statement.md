# WITNESS STATEMENT

**Mohave Market — Loss Prevention Department**
**Statement of:** Marcus T. Park
**Position:** Loss Prevention Officer
**Date of Statement:** March 16, 2026, 09:40 hrs *(written the morning following the incident)*
**Re:** Incident of March 15, 2026, approximately 20:30–20:50 hrs
**Subject:** Jamie M. Reyes

---

My name is Marcus Park. I am the on-duty Loss Prevention Officer at Mohave Market. I have worked in this position for approximately fourteen (14) months. I am writing this statement at the request of Mohave County Attorney's Office.

On the evening of March 15, 2026, I was on shift in the LP office at the front of the store. The LP office has a wall-mounted CCTV monitor that displays a four-camera split view rotating across the eight store cameras. The monitor screen is approximately 19 inches.

At around 8:30 PM, I noticed an individual in the electronics aisle on Camera 4. The person was wearing a dark jacket with a hood up and had a black backpack. I watched for what felt like a few minutes. **At one point I saw the person reach toward the earbud display, and shortly after I saw a hand near the backpack. I cannot say for certain on the camera that I saw the items go into the backpack — the angle and the size of the screen made it hard to tell — but it looked to me like that's what happened, and I had a strong feeling about it.**

I continued to watch as the person walked toward the front of the store. They walked past the registers without stopping. As they approached the exit doors, I left the office to intercept them.

I caught up with the person in the parking lot, about fifteen feet from the front doors. I identified myself as Loss Prevention and asked them to come back inside. The person — who I now know to be Jamie Reyes — complied without resistance.

Inside the LP office, I asked Reyes to set their backpack down. I did not search the backpack myself, but it was in plain view next to where Reyes was seated. I called BCPD and waited for the responding officer to arrive.

When Officer Whitfield arrived, the backpack was opened in his presence and the earbuds and charger were inside. Both had Mohave Market price tags on them.

**I am one hundred percent (100%) certain that the person I saw on the camera in the electronics aisle is the same person I detained in the parking lot and the same person who was in the LP office.** I did not lose sight of the person for any meaningful length of time between the camera observation and the parking lot stop.

I would like to note that the store's full surveillance footage from that evening should be available — our system retains footage for 30 days.

I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct.

**/s/ Marcus T. Park**

# AFFIDAVIT OF PROBABLE CAUSE

**State of Arizona, County of Mohave**
**Case No.:** CR2026-00428
**Affiant:** Officer Daniel Whitfield, Bullhead City Police Department, Badge #2147

---

I, Daniel Whitfield, being first duly sworn upon oath, depose and state:

1. I am a sworn peace officer with the Bullhead City Police Department, and have served in that capacity for approximately seven (7) years. I am familiar with Arizona statutes governing theft and shoplifting offenses.

2. On March 15, 2026 at approximately 20:35 hrs, I was dispatched to Mohave Market at 1234 AZ-95, Bullhead City, AZ, in reference to a shoplifting in progress.

3. Upon arrival, I spoke with Marcus Park, employed as Loss Prevention Officer at Mohave Market. **Mr. Park stated that he personally observed the Defendant, Jamie M. Reyes, conceal merchandise belonging to Mohave Market in a black backpack and exit the store past the registers without paying for said merchandise.**

4. Mr. Park further stated that he physically detained the Defendant in the parking lot immediately following the Defendant's exit from the store, and held the Defendant in the front-of-store office until the arrival of police.

5. Upon arrival, I personally observed in the front-of-store office a black backpack containing one (1) sealed package of wireless earbuds and one (1) USB-C charger, both bearing Mohave Market price tags totaling $114.98. The backpack was in the immediate possession of the Defendant.

6. After being advised of Miranda rights, the Defendant agreed to speak with me. **In the course of that interview, the Defendant made statements that, in my professional judgment, constitute admissions to the offense of shoplifting.** The substance of these statements is reflected in my interview notes.

7. Based upon the foregoing, I have probable cause to believe that the Defendant, Jamie M. Reyes, committed the offense of Shoplifting in violation of A.R.S. § 13-1805(A)(1), a Class 1 Misdemeanor.

I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.

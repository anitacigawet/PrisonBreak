## CRIMINAL COMPLAINT

The undersigned, being duly sworn, deposes and states upon information and belief that on or about **March 15, 2026**, in the City of Bullhead City, County of Mohave, State of Arizona, the above-named Defendant did commit the following offense:

### COUNT 1 — SHOPLIFTING

In violation of **Arizona Revised Statutes § 13-1805(A)(1)** and § 13-1805(H), a Class 1 Misdemeanor, in that Defendant, while in an establishment in which merchandise was displayed for sale, knowingly obtained said merchandise of another with the intent to deprive that person of such merchandise, by removing said merchandise from the immediate display or other place of safekeeping without paying the purchase price therefor.

**Specifically:** that the Defendant did remove from Mohave Market (a retail establishment located at 1234 AZ-95, Bullhead City, Arizona) the following merchandise without paying the purchase price:

- One (1) pair of wireless earbuds, retail price $89.99
- One (1) USB-C charger, retail price $24.99

**Total alleged value:** $114.98
